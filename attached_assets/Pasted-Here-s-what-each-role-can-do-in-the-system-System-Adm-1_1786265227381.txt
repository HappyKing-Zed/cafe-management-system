Here's what each role can do in the system:
________________________________________
🔴 System Administrator (admin@)
Full access to everything — the only role that can change system configuration, manage API keys, and access all modules without restriction.
________________________________________
🟠 Restaurant Owner (owner@)
Same as Admin except cannot modify low-level system configuration. Oversees the entire business: users, staff, menus, finances, inventory, and all reports.
________________________________________
🟡 Manager (manager@)
Day-to-day operations head. Can:
•	Create employees, branches, and restaurants
•	View & manage employees, branches, Restaurants
•	Run and export all business reports
•	Full control of orders, kitchen, tables, and POS
•	Approve/reject refunds and purchase orders
•	View billing, payments, and shift summaries
•	View inventory and approve stock adjustments
________________________________________
🟢 Order Coordinator (coordinator@)
Routes orders between front-of-house and the kitchen. Can:
•	Create and assign orders on behalf of waiters
•	Update order status and table status
•	View the kitchen board
•	Access the POS terminal to place orders
________________________________________
🔵 Waiter (waiter1@, waiter2@)
Takes customer orders at the table. Can:
•	Use the POS terminal to create and submit orders
•	Update and cancel their own orders
•	Mark orders as served when food arrives
•	View and update table status (seat/clear a table)
________________________________________
🟣 Chef (chef@)
Manages the kitchen workflow. Can:
•	View the kitchen board with incoming tickets
•	Accept orders → mark as Preparing → mark as Ready
•	Update kitchen station assignments
•	View order details (items, notes, modifiers)
________________________________________
⚪ Cashier (cashier@)
Handles all payments and closing orders. Can:
•	Open and close a cash drawer shift
•	Generate bills from completed orders
•	Process payments (cash, card, mobile)
•	Request and view refunds
•	Access the POS for walk-up/counter orders
•	View daily financial reports
________________________________________
🟤 Inventory Manager / Store Keeper (storekeeper@)
Full control of the stockroom. Can:
•	Manage ingredients, units of measure, and batches
•	Create and manage warehouses and stock levels
•	Add, edit, and evaluate suppliers
•	Create purchase requisitions and purchase orders
•	Receive goods (goods receipt) and post to stock
•	Record stock adjustments, transfers between warehouses, and waste
•	View inventory reports and export data
•	Read recipes (to understand ingredient linkages)
