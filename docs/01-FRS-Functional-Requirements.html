Functional Requirements Specification (FRS)

System: Jima Aba Jifar — Cafe & Restaurant Management System
Document: FRS v1.0  ·  Date: August 12, 2026
Related documents: SRS (02), SDD (03), User Manual (04), Handoff & Deployment (05), Vercel Guide (06)

1. Purpose

This document defines the functional requirements of the Jima Aba Jifar Restaurant Management System — a full-stack web application that manages the daily operations of a cafe & restaurant: ordering, kitchen workflow, payments, inventory, staff, branches, and reporting.

2. User Roles

RoleDescription
AdminFull system access; manages all data, users, and configuration.
OwnerBusiness owner; same operational visibility as manager, including analytics dashboard.
ManagerRuns daily operations; manages menu, staff, tables, branches, inventory, and reports.
CoordinatorConfirms incoming orders and assigns them to chefs; can cancel orders.
WaiterTakes orders at tables (POS), adds items to open orders, tracks order progress.
ChefWorks the kitchen board: accepts, prepares, and marks orders ready.
CashierCollects payments, closes orders, views sales and items-purchased reports.
StorekeeperManages stock levels, receives goods, handles item requests and adjustments.

3. Functional Requirements

3.1 Authentication & Authorization

IDRequirement
FR-A01Users log in with e-mail and password; the system returns a JWT access token.
FR-A02Every protected API call requires a valid Authorization: Bearer token.
FR-A03Menu entries and pages are restricted per role (role-based access control).
FR-A04Passwords are stored hashed (bcrypt), never in plain text.

3.2 Orders & POS

IDRequirement
FR-O01Waiters create orders through a POS screen: pick a table (or take-away), select menu items by category, set quantities and notes.
FR-O02Orders follow the lifecycle: pending → confirmed → preparing → ready → served → paid, with cancelled possible before "ready".
FR-O03The coordinator confirms a pending order and assigns a chef to it.
FR-O04Waiters may append items to any order that is not yet paid or cancelled.
FR-O05The orders list shows table, chef, item count, time, total, payment method, and status; rows open a detail view.
FR-O06Orders can be filtered by status, waiter, table, item, date, time, and payment method.
FR-O07The list and open detail view refresh automatically every 10 seconds.

3.3 Kitchen

IDRequirement
FR-K01The kitchen board shows confirmed orders queued per chef, with tabs per chef.
FR-K02Chefs move orders through accept → preparing → ready.
FR-K03The Order Progress Board displays four live columns: Requested, Received & Confirmed, In Progress, Completed (ready only).
FR-K04Orders waiting more than 20 minutes are visually flagged.

3.4 Payments

IDRequirement
FR-P01Cashiers record payments against served orders with method cash, card, or wallet, including change given.
FR-P02Paying an order marks it paid and frees its table.
FR-P03Daily reports summarize revenue, transaction counts, and per-method breakdowns.

3.5 Menu Management

IDRequirement
FR-M01Managers maintain menu categories and items (name, price, description, availability, preparation time).
FR-M02Unavailable items are hidden from the POS.

3.6 Inventory & Store

IDRequirement
FR-I01Inventory items track unit, current stock, minimum stock, reorder point, unit cost, category, and expiry date.
FR-I02Low-stock alerts flag items below minimum (critical when at or below half the minimum, or zero).
FR-I03Suppliers and purchase orders are managed with status tracking.
FR-I04Stock adjustments record additions, deductions, and waste with reasons and the acting user.
FR-I05Staff submit item requests; managers/storekeepers approve, issue, and track them, with low-stock / expiry badges shown on request rows.

3.7 Staff, Branches & Tables

IDRequirement
FR-S01Managers create and manage staff accounts with roles and branch assignment.
FR-S02Restaurants and branches are managed hierarchically (restaurant → branches).
FR-S03Tables have number, capacity, section, and live status (available / occupied).

3.8 Dashboards & Reports

IDRequirement
FR-D01Admin/Owner/Manager see a combined dashboard: today's KPIs (sales, orders, average order value, in-progress, low stock, stock value), sales trend, top sellers, profitability, and kitchen performance — on a single page. KPI cards open detail popups.
FR-D02Reports offer per-area export tabs (Order Board, Menu, Inventory, Item Requested, Staff, Branches) with filters and Excel / PDF export.
FR-D03Cashiers see a reduced report view: Sales and Items Purchased only, filterable by type and date, exportable to Excel / PDF.
FR-D04Role-specific landing: waiters, coordinators, and cashiers land on Orders/POS instead of the dashboard.

3.9 Notifications

IDRequirement
FR-N01The system generates notifications targeted at roles or specific users (e.g. new order, order ready) with read tracking.

4. Business Rules

  • An order's total is the sum of its items (quantity × unit price) plus any service charge percentage.
  • Only pending orders can be confirmed; only served orders can be paid.
  • Cancelling is allowed only while an order is pending, confirmed, or preparing.
  • Data is scoped by branch where applicable (orders, tables, staff, stock).
